**"It is not the employer who pays the wages. Employers only handle the money. It is the customer who pays the wages."** _给员工发工资的不是雇主，雇主只是负责过手金钱的人，真正支付薪水的是顾客。_
**"There is only one boss. The customer. And he can fire everybody in the company from the chairman on down, simply by spending his money somewhere else."** _公司只有一个老板，那就是顾客。他只需要把钱花在其他地方，就能把公司从董事长到基层员工的每个人都解雇掉。_
